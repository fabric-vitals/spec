# Trademark policy (draft)

**Status:** Draft policy, pending legal review. Nothing in this file is legal advice, and no registration is claimed.

"**Fabric Vitals**" and "**Vitals Score**" are claimed as marks of **datacenternetwork.ai**. The specification text is licensed separately under CC BY 4.0 ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)); that license covers the text, not the names.

## Why the names are protected

A health score is useful only if the name means one thing. If a modified method could call itself Fabric Vitals, two "Vitals Scores" for the same fabric could disagree, and neither would mean what the published Rulebook says. The names are protected so that they always refer to the published method.

## Permitted use

You may, without asking:
- state that a product, service or tool **"implements Fabric Vitals vX.Y"** when it follows the published Rulebook and spec-sheet rules of that version, without modification;
- refer to Fabric Vitals, the Vitals Score and this specification in articles, papers, talks, reviews and comparisons, including critical ones;
- use the names in issues, pull requests and discussions about the method.

When you state that you implement Fabric Vitals, name the specification version and the Rulebook version you implement.

## Not permitted

- Using "Fabric Vitals" or "Vitals Score" for a **modified method**: different aggregation, different critical conditions, different coverage rules, values changed outside the published Rulebook, or a spec sheet that carries a class-preset name without matching the preset.
- **Implying endorsement**, certification or partnership by datacenternetwork.ai or by any future steward, unless it exists in writing.
- Using the names, or confusingly similar names, as the name of a product, company or domain.

You are free to build a modified method from the CC BY 4.0 text. Give it a different name, and credit Fabric Vitals as its source.

## What may follow

A **compatibility programme** may follow: a published test suite and a process by which implementations can show that they produce the same score records as the reference behaviour. Until it exists, "implements Fabric Vitals vX.Y" is a self-declaration.

## Stewardship

If stewardship of Fabric Vitals moves to a neutral body, the marks move with it ([`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)).
